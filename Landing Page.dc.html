Why we started Almaya

Eitan and I both had successful independent college consultancies that we ran on our own. What we kept seeing with so many parents was that they needed access to one-on-one, high-level advising in a transparent way, with vetted, high-trust advisors.

Other consultancies online were either inflating prices for hours of time or listing tons of advisors: people who posed as college consultants but didn’t have meaningful, vetted backgrounds with experience in writing or tutoring. We’ve done all that work for you, so you can just do what you need to do.

Razi Hecker, Co-founder
